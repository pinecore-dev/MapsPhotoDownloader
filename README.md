# Maps Photo Downloader

Chrome extension that shows the photos of a Google Maps place in a side panel so you can pick the ones you want and download them at full size.

Meant for business owners who want their own photos back. Only download photos you have rights to, and keep in mind Google's terms.

## Install

1. Unzip it.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions` and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the folder.

## How it works

Open a place in Maps and scroll through its photos. The extension picks them up as they load and lists them in the panel. Files are saved as `MapsPhotoDownloaderXXX1.jpg` in the Downloads folder.

Nothing is sent anywhere: no account, no server, no tracking.

Languages: Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, German.
